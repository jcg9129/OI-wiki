**Graph theory** is a branch of mathematics, and graphs are the main research object of graph theory. A **graph** is a figure composed of several given vertices and edges connecting two vertices; such a figure is usually used to describe some specific relationship among certain things. Vertices are used to represent things, and an edge connecting two vertices is used to indicate that the two things have this relationship.
