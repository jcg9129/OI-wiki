## Welcome to **OI Wiki**! [![GitHub watchers](https://img.shields.io/github/watchers/OI-wiki/OI-wiki.svg?style=social&label=Watch)](https://github.com/OI-wiki/OI-wiki)  [![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/OI-wiki/OI-wiki.svg?style=social&label=Stars)](https://github.com/OI-wiki/OI-wiki)

[![Word Art](images/wordArt.webp)](https://github.com/OI-wiki/OI-wiki)

**OI** (Olympiad in Informatics) originated in China in 1984 and is one of the five major high-school academic olympiads.

**ICPC** (International Collegiate Programming Contest), organized by the ICPC Foundation, is the most influential collegiate computer-programming competition. Because ACM used to sponsor the contest, many people are also used to calling it the ACM contest.

**OI Wiki** strives to be a free, open, and continuously updated knowledge hub for **competitive programming**, where everyone can find interesting and practical contest-related knowledge. We have prepared fundamentals, common problem types, solution ideas, and commonly used tools to help you learn the knowledge involved in competitive programming more quickly and deeply.

This project was inspired by [CTF Wiki](https://ctf-wiki.org/), and referenced many materials during its writing, to which we express our thanks.
