## Event introduction

### ICPC

**ICPC** (International Collegiate Programming Contest) is hosted by the ICPC Foundation and is the most influential collegiate computer competition. Because ACM used to sponsor this competition, many people are also used to calling it the ACM contest.

ICPC is mainly divided into two parts: the Regionals and the World Finals.

Official website: <https://icpc.global>

### CCPC

Official website: <https://ccpc.io>

The China Collegiate Programming Contest.

A notable difference from ICPC is that many schools do not reimburse for it.

## Format introduction

Generally, a team of three people uses one machine, with multiple submission opportunities during the contest. The contest judges in real time and returns results; if a submission's result is wrong, there is a 20-minute penalty, and the more wrong attempts there are, the longer the added penalty time. A problem earns points only after all data points are correct. The contest ranking is judged by the number of problems solved; among those with the same number solved, it is judged by total time. The total time is the sum of the time for each problem. The time for each problem is the number of minutes from the start of the contest to solving that problem, plus the penalty time for that problem.

In some ICPC-related events, the scoreboard is frozen one hour before the contest ends; submissions and rankings after the freeze cannot be seen by other contestants.

In ICPC-related events, contestants are allowed to bring a certain amount of printed material.

Besides ICPC and CCPC, many other competitions also adopt this format, such as LeetCode weekly contests and national programming competitions, and Nowcoder's beginner/practice/challenge contests.

## Season schedule

-   ICPC/CCPC online contests (late August to early September)
-   ICPC/CCPC regional contests (late September to late November)
-   ICPC EC Final / CCPC Final (mid-December)
-   ICPC World Finals (April to June of the following year)

## Training guide

### Multi-University joint training

Training contests held on [HDU OJ](http://acm.hdu.edu.cn) during the summer. They have prizes, the problem quality is high, and over many years of accumulation they have amassed rich resources.

Keyword for searching in the OJ: `Multi-University Training Contest`.

### Domestic regional contests

Curated problem sets can be found on [Virtual Judge](https://vjudge.net/).
